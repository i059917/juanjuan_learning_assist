13. They ____________ (not have) an English class today.

## 第 3 页 · 综合练习（have/has 作普通动词 · 否定 · 情态动词 · 单复数交叉）

每题只有一个空格，请在横线上填入括号中动词的正确形式。

1. He ____________ (have) a fever today.
2. They ____________ (have) a meeting every Monday.
3. My aunt ____________ (have) a new dictionary.
4. There ____________ (be) a lot of students in the classroom.
5. She ____________ (not have) a car.
6. ____________ (do) your sister have a bike?
7. He ____________ (not play) basketball on Sunday.
8. The bird ____________ (not fly) south in summer.
9. She ____________ (not do) like apples.
10. He ____________ (do) not want to go.
11. We ____________ (can) swim in the river in summer.
12. You ____________ (must) finish your homework before dinner.
13. Tom ____________ (do) his homework and his sister ____________ (do) the dishes.
14. The cat ____________ (sleep) on the sofa and the birds ____________ (sing) in the tree.
15. My father usually ____________ (drive) to work, but today he ____________ (take) the bus.
16. ____________ (do) your brother watch TV in the evening?
